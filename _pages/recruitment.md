# Open positions

## Post-docs 
The McGraw Lab is currently recruiting post-doctoral scholars to work on projects related to our refractory seizure models.
Two Fellowship opportunities are available:
1) Post-doctoral Research Fellowship in Refractory Seizure Disorders and Treatment Innovation
2) Post-doctoral Research Fellowship in Genetic Epilepsy

_Required_
• PhD in Neuroscience, Pharmacology, Medical Genetics, Chemical Biology, or a related discipline by the start date. 
• Record of achievement during graduate studies as evidenced by first-authored articles in peer-reviewed journals +/- independent grants 
• Experience working with animal models (mouse, or zebrafish) (preferred)

_Compensation_
• Salary starting at $61,000, commensurate with candidate’s background and experience. 
• Comprehensive benefits package (postdocs.northwestern.edu)
• The position is offered for one year with extensions of up to three years. Fellows are highly encouraged to apply for independent extramural funding after Y1.

Please send a cover letter describing your interest in the Fellowship along with your CV and contact information for three references by email to [Dr. Chris McGraw](mailto:Christopher.mcgraw@northwestern.edu). 

## Graduate students 
Dr. McGraw is currently a member of NUIN, CGM, and is amenable to joining other graduate programs as necessary. Typical rotation projects may be wet-lab, involving animal models of epilepsy (zebrafish, mouse), molecular biology (methods development), or dry lab (data analysis). With appropriate co-mentorship, engineering projects (lab automation) are also of great interest. Applicants should send me an [email](mailto:christopher.mcgraw@northwestern.edu) to apply. 

## Research tech/lab manager
The McGraw Lab is recruiting for an experienced lab manager, ideally with 2+ years of animal research (mouse, EEG), and lab management experience. Please see the NUJobs posting to apply and send me an [email](mailto:christopher.mcgraw@northwestern.edu).

## Medical students
We have openings for talented and motivated medical students! Ideal applicants will have a demonstrated interest in epilepsy, and can commit to at least 2 semesters. Please send me an [email](mailto:christopher.mcgraw@northwestern.edu) to apply.
